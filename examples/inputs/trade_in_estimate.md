# Feature request: Trade-in estimate in phone checkout

**Requested by:** Device Commerce product team
**Brand:** Nimbus Wireless
**Platforms:** app and web

## Background
Customers who upgrade their phone often abandon checkout at the trade-in step because they don't know what their current device is worth until after they ship it. Trade-in value is one of the top questions in upgrade calls to care. We want to give customers an instant estimate before they commit.

## What we want
- Customers should be able to enter their current device model and condition during phone checkout and see an estimated trade-in value.
- The estimate must be applied as a credit preview in the order summary.
- Customers must be able to skip the trade-in step and continue checkout.
- The estimate must come from the existing Trade-In Pricing Service.
- Show a disclaimer that the final value is confirmed after device inspection.
- The estimate should load fast and the flow should be easy to use.

## Success
We want to increase checkout completion for upgrade orders and reduce trade-in related calls to care. Track the funnel from trade-in step viewed to estimate shown to order placed.

## Out of scope
- Changing the device inspection process.
- Trade-in for tablets and wearables (phones only for now).

## Notes
Not sure yet if Nimbus Prepaid customers are eligible. We may want to A/B test the placement of the trade-in step.
