# Feature request: AI chat assistant entry point on the bill page

**Requested by:** Digital Care product team
**Brand:** Nimbus Wireless and Nimbus Prepaid
**Platforms:** app and web

## Background
Billing questions are the largest driver of care calls in the first week after a bill is issued. Most of them are simple, such as why the bill went up or when a promo credit ends. The AI chat assistant already exists in the Help section, but very few customers find it from the bill page.

## What we want
- Add a "Questions about your bill?" entry point on the bill page that opens the AI chat assistant.
- When opened from the bill page, the assistant must receive the current bill period and account context so the customer does not have to re-explain.
- The assistant must never display full payment card or bank account numbers.
- Customers must be able to escalate to a live care agent from the chat at any time.
- Customers should be able to rate each answer with thumbs up or thumbs down.
- The entry point should be intuitive and the assistant should respond quickly.

## Success
Reduce billing-related calls to care and increase the share of bill-page chat sessions resolved without an agent. Track entry point views, chat opens, messages, escalations and ratings.

## Out of scope
- Changing the assistant's underlying model or knowledge base.
- Proactive push notifications about the bill.

## Notes
Legal needs to review how bill data is shared with the assistant. Unclear whether account sub-users (non-owners) should see the entry point.
